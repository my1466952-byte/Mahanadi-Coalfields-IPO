## About Mahanadi Coalfields Limited

Established in 1992, Mahanadi Coalfields Limited is headquartered in Odisha. The company primarily operates in coal mining and supplies coal to industries such as power generation, cement and sponge iron. It is one of the major contributors to India's domestic coal production.

## IPO Objectives and Issue Structure

The Mahanadi Coalfields IPO is entirely an Offer for Sale (OFS). Under this issue, Coal India Limited will sell approximately 66.18 crore equity shares, representing a 10% stake in the company.

Therefore, the company will not receive any proceeds from the IPO, as the funds will go to the selling shareholder.
